People Search

Find the right people. Just describe them.

Describe who you’re looking for in plain language. Redrob AI asks a couple of sharp follow-ups, then searches and returns real people — ranked by fit — with roles, work history, and verified contact details. No boolean strings to learn. Filters are there if you want them.

Company Search

Know the company before you reach out.

The same conversation, pointed at companies. Describe what you’re after — refine by industry, location, size, or funding stage if you like — and Redrob AI returns matching companies, each with a full profile: what they do, their financials, and the people who run them.

Job Search

2 crore+ live jobs. Matched to you.

Tell Redrob AI what you want, and it actively searches more than 2 crore live jobs — refreshed daily, pulled from every source: LinkedIn, Naukri, and virtually any role posted anywhere on the internet, across the globe. Full-time, internships, freelance, side hustles. Filter precisely if you want, and get listings matched to your profile, each linked to the company behind it. For a job seeker, there is no more complete view of the market.

Every kind of opportunity, in one place. Nothing stays hidden.
